Water sports plan · January to June 2027

Make your catchment swim, January to June

Why: swimming jumps from 3% to 11.5% of our turnover in March and April, and the sheet's two priorities are to win the summer learners and keep them next year. Swim After Dark

Our store events move to the night (or very early morning) where the pool allows: music, underwater lights and a DJ outside the pool, with people around and the energy of a festival. Think of it as a music festival, in a swimming pool.

  • Partner with premium pools; timed sessions with a beginner lane and an advanced lane
  • Underwater photos and short videos: content for the city pages and the creators
  • Limited-edition event swim caps
  • Money: from the store events line (no new budget); the pool, the DJ and the lights to agree per city

Business outcome: brand desirability, young people in the pool, and content.

AcquisitionParticipationFebruary 2027

Free coaching for every child

In February, every child who comes to a Decathlon store gets a voucher for 5 free coaching classes at one of our partner pools (AOPs). It brings families in before the season and fills the partner pools.

  • The voucher at the till and on WhatsApp, with the child's registration: this is how we count registrations and cost per lead
  • Redeemed at the partner pools near the store; the coach logs each class, so we see first swims and the second and fourth swim
  • Water safety is led by qualified instructors; 5 classes are a start, never sold as learning to swim
  • Money: to agree with the partners (classes as part of the partnership, or a fee per class); not in the budget yet

Business outcome: families become repeat customers for swimwear, goggles, caps and training gear.

The Aqua Zumba standee: join our WhatsApp community

A die-cut standee at the entrance and in the swim aisle of every store: the store's Aqua Zumba and water events with their dates, and one QR code. Scanning it registers the customer for the event and adds them to the store's WhatsApp community, so we can bring them back for the next swim, event and sale.

  • One standee a store, the dates changed for each store's events (Aqua Zumba is in the water sports sheet's event ideas, monthly from March)
  • The QR goes to the store's WhatsApp community: the sheet's targets: 100 members a store in February, 350 in March, 400 in April, 500 by May (Rajkot 300)
  • Same look as our water campaign: blue water, yellow splash, the swimmer
  • Money: printing to add to the in-store budget (not in the water sheet)

Business outcome: more people in the WhatsApp community, who come back for the second and fourth swim and buy their gear with us.
