    Winter + Travel

    48% of winter turnover lands in Q4. Five moves to win it:

    1. Influencers. 20 trek videos with route names and demos (1.5 Mn views), 10 footwear UGC videos (1 Mn) and creators showing lowest-price best-value offers. A further 40 influencers push quick commerce, targeting 5 Mn views.
    2. In-store merch. A winter window with signage in every store, with a winning-points checklist.
    3. In-store challenge. NH500 models to lift ABV.
    4. Affiliation. 50 travel agencies onboarded as affiliates.
    5. Travel meetups. Store meetups once 300 members are onboarded. Stores open an hour early to catch early footfall.

    Gift a Sport

    Festive gifting runs from Dussehra to Christmas with ready-made edits: gifts under ₹999, gifts under ₹1,999, Decathlon's Choice, gifts for footballers and gifts for runners.

    Adajan, Surat opens 20 Dec

    A large-format store for Surat city and the towns around it: Dumas, Vesu, Adajan, Piplod and Vapi. Free gifts with purchase on opening days, 7 free Deca Runners classes from 20 Dec to 16 Jan, and twice the usual marketing spend in Q1 to build the habit.
